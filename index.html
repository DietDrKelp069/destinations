Destinations: Travel Journal

Privacy policy, subscription terms and support. Last updated November 1, 2026.

Privacy policy

Destinations collects no data. Everything you add (trips, photos, journal entries, places, expenses, packing lists and settings) is saved only on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • No accounts. You don't sign up or log in.
  • No tracking, ads or analytics. The app has no third-party code that watches what you do.
  • Location is used only when you tap the GPS button to fill in where you are, and stays on your phone. Place search uses Apple Maps, which Apple runs under its own privacy policy.
  • Photos you pick are copied into the app's own storage on your phone. The app doesn't ask for access to your whole photo library.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account.

Deleting the app deletes everything it saved. Your data is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which are protected by Apple.

Children: the app collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes (for example if online sync is added), this page will be updated before the app changes, and the app will ask before sending anything off your phone.

Subscription terms

Destinations is free. Destinations Plus unlocks every look and the countries section, and is available as:

  • Plus Monthly: $2.99 per month, renews automatically.
  • Plus Yearly: $9.99 per year, renews automatically.
  • Plus Lifetime: $19.99 once, never renews.

Prices are in US dollars and may differ in other countries. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless it is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from "Manage subscription" on the app's Plus page. Look packs ($0.99 each) are one-time purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Use of the app is also covered by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.
